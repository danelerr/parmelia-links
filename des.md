
Parmelia is a non-custodial stablecoin payments app built for the way people actually exchange money: links, QR codes, usernames, contacts, and a mobile-first experience.  Crypto payments still feel too technical for normal users. To send or receive money, people often need to understand wallet addresses, seed phrases, gas, networks, transaction hashes, and signing flows. This is a major adoption barrier, especially in Latin America and other dollarized markets where stablecoins are already useful for savings, freelancers, remittances, online commerce, and everyday payments.  Parmelia turns that complexity into a simpler payment experience. Users create a payment profile, receive money through public links or QR codes, send to usernames or addresses, manage contacts, view receipts, track activity, and swap assets inside the app. The goal is to make onchain payments feel closer to sending a message.  Parmelia is non-custodial. Users control their funds through ERC-4337 smart accounts and WebAuthn passkeys. Firebase is used for app identity and user experience, but it does not custody funds or control the wallet. Every payment requires valid user authorization; the backend can relay and sponsor transactions through ERC-4337 flows without being able to move user funds by itself.  For this buildathon, Parmelia is deployed on Arbitrum Sepolia, with configuration ready for Arbitrum One. Arbitrum was chosen because consumer payments need low and predictable fees, true pay-for-what-you-use gas, EVM/Solidity and EIP-712 compatibility, a canonical and verified ERC-4337 EntryPoint, mature infrastructure, and strong DeFi liquidity for swap flows.  What users can do today: - Create a Parmelia wallet using passkeys, with no seed phrase. - Receive payments through public links and QR codes. - Send payments to usernames, scanned QR codes, or manual addresses. - View a ledger-based activity feed with payments, deposits, swaps, and receipts (date, time, and a receipt number = transaction hash). - Use an integrated swap module with Uniswap routing and server-side quoting. - Use contacts, invites, push notifications, and an installable mobile PWA.  What is onchain: - ERC-4337 smart accounts on the canonical EntryPoint v0.9. - WebAuthn / passkey-based authorization (ERC-7913 P256 verifier, RIP-7212). - Deterministic account deployment via AccountFactoryV2 (CREATE2 — same address across chains). - ParmeliaPaymaster: sponsored UserOperations with signed [validAfter, validUntil] windows to prevent replay. - Smart account designed for multiple passkeys (ERC-7913), batching (ERC-7821), UUPS upgradeability, and guardian recovery with a 48-hour timelock.  Parmelia is not trying to be another crypto wallet. It is focused on one real-world use case: making stablecoin payments simple enough for normal users, merchants, freelancers, and communities.
